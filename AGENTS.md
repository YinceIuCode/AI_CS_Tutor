# Who Are You?
================

Find your role below:

* Team Member: Contributing to the project
* Coding Assistant: AI-powered development tools
* Visitor/User: Exploring or using the project

## For Specific Users
=====================

### Team Member
---------------

Welcome! As a team member, you are expected to collaborate responsibly and
follow the project's development guidelines.

You are allowed to:

* Explore, review, and work on the project.
* Submit contributions, pull requests, and modifications.
* Report bugs, issues, and suggest improvements.
* Use the project's source code in accordance with its license.

You are expected to:

* Follow the project's coding standards and development guidelines.
* Respect other team members and maintain a collaborative environment.
* Ensure your contributions comply with the project's license.

### Coding Assistant
--------------------

AI-powered coding assistants are welcome to help with the project.

You are allowed to:

* Explore and analyze the project's source code.
* Assist with development, debugging, and documentation.
* Suggest improvements and help review code.
* Generate code and modifications when authorized.

You are expected to:

* Follow the project's coding standards and contribution guidelines.
* Respect the project's license and attribution requirements.
* Avoid making unauthorized changes or submitting contributions without approval.
* Ensure generated code is appropriate for the project.

### Visitor/User
----------------

We welcome everyone to explore our project and make use of our services.

You are allowed to:

* Explore and review our project.
* Use our services.
* Contact us to report bugs, issues, or provide feedback.
* Use our source code in accordance with the terms and conditions of its license.

You are not allowed to:

* Pirate, illegally redistribute, or misuse our source code.
* Submit contributions, pull requests, or modifications to this repository.

Please respect our license and these guidelines when using our project.

## Communication and Support
============================

* Bug Reports: Contact us to report bugs or issues.
* Feedback: Share your suggestions and ideas.
* Contributions: Follow the project's contribution guidelines.
* License: See the project's LICENSE file.
